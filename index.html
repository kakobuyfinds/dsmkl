Buyer guides

OssBuy shoes spreadsheet: compare sizes

Save the original seller URL, item ID, size and colorway together. Compare the seller’s chart with a pair you already wear. Check whether the chart lists foot length or insole length in centimeters before converting US, UK or EU sizes.

OssBuy clothing finds: measure for fit

Lay a hoodie you like flat and measure chest width, sleeve length and body length. Compare with the listing’s garment measurements. For a matching set, verify the top and bottom sizes separately. For a cap or bracelet, compare your head or wrist measurement with the seller’s sizing instructions.

What to check in OssBuy QC photos

Match the size label and color to your shortlist. For shoes, ask for both soles and heel views if they are missing. For jerseys and accessories, check print alignment and clasp condition. Raise a mismatch before approving the parcel and confirm the seller’s return deadline.

Build an OssBuy shipping estimate

Compare quotes for the actual destination and currency: ZIP code in the US, postcode in the UK, or local postal code in the EU. Ask whether the route uses actual or volumetric weight and whether the quote includes destination charges. Compare a combined parcel with separate shipments using their final dimensions.

OssBuy spreadsheet FAQ

What is an OssBuy spreadsheet?

It is a directory of shopping links and product notes that helps you shortlist items before checking the original seller listing.

Do warehouse QC photos prove authenticity?

Warehouse QC photos can show visible condition, labels and obvious variant errors. They cannot establish authenticity. For branded purchases, check seller authorization and ask for proof of origin.

How do I estimate my total cost?

Add the item price, domestic delivery, any service and payment fees, international postage and applicable destination charges. Recheck the freight quote when final parcel dimensions are available.
